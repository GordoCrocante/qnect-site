Player 1 · The bubble

You moved to a new city. The city did not move to you.

Everyone arrives with plans: locals, language, a life here. Months later the friends are still from home, the Spanish still stops at the coffee order, and the city is a backdrop you commute through.

Nobody chooses the bubble. It is just comfortable, and it has no exit. Apps for meeting people make it worse: more screens, more swiping, nobody shows up.

Our exit

Put strangers in a small team. Give them one real thing to make, a deadline, and a night where everyone shows what they made. Friendship is the side effect.

Level select · How a challenge works

Four levels. Every challenge has the same four.

Level 1

Team

3 to 6 strangers

Come alone or with friends. We build the teams. Mixed levels on purpose: the A1 gets dragged along, the C1 learns to shut up and film.

Level 2

Goal

Make one real thing

Something you can show a room. In Round 1: a sarcastic Spanish drama trailer. Shot on your phones. Bad on purpose is fine. Boring is not.

Level 3

Deadline

7 days

One week. Enough to argue, plan, shoot and panic. The deadline is what makes six strangers actually meet up on a Tuesday.

Level 4

Premiere

One night, one rooftop

Every team shows what they made in front of everyone. The room votes. The prize is the story you tell afterwards.
